Trend following backtest

Python, pandas, NumPy. 2026. Code and full report

A backtest of the 50/200 day moving average crossover on SPY, written from scratch in Python. Trades go through the day after a signal, every trade has a cost, and the engine has unit tests. I picked the parameters on 2000 to 2017 and tested on 2018 to 2026.

It didn't beat buy and hold on the test years.

It looked a lot better on the training years than the test years, so a good chunk of the edge was overfitting.

Household size prediction

Python, LightGBM, scikit‑learn, R. 2026. Class Kaggle competition, team of three

Class Kaggle competition where we predicted how many people live in a household from their Amazon purchases, scored on RMSE. Since the answer is a small capped count, we treated it as classification and used the expected value of the predicted probabilities, which did better than regressing on it directly.

TF‑IDF on the product titles worked much better than our hand picked keywords. There were more features than rows, so ridge regression was solved in dual form. The final model was a stack of seven and placed 3rd out of 23 teams.
